# Conventions for identification

Rulings on names that reading alone does not settle. The harness appends this file to the system
prompt. Each item gives the date of the ruling. An ID given here may be used without a search, but
the entry is still read and the dates still checked before it goes into an answer (guideline,
sec. 2.3).

## «ابن فهد» as the source of a date or a notice

Three members of the family wrote on the people of Mecca: al-Taqī Muḥammad (AIND-D09211),
his son al-Najm ʿUmar (AIND-D05979), and ʿUmar's son al-ʿIzz ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz (AIND-D03985). When
the text names one of them («التقي بن فهد», «النجم بن فهد»), that is the person. When it says only
«ابن فهد» («أرخه ابن فهد», «ذكره ابن فهد»), decide by the year of death of the person recorded
(ruling of batch B54; rule 60 a for the plural):

| Case | Answer | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| «في معجمه» | AIND-D09211 | `high` |
| Death after 885 | AIND-D03985 | `high` |
| Death after 871, up to 885 | AIND-D05979 | `medium` |
| Death in or before 871, or unknown | AIND-D09211 | `low` |

- «أرخهما», «أرخهم ثلاثتهم ابن فهد», said of several persons at once: use the latest of their
  deaths for all of them.
- «النجم بن فهد … في معجم أبيه»: the author is al-Najm ʿUmar; the book is his father's.
- Ibn Fahd as a student («سمع منه ابن فهد», «لقيه ابن فهد»): decide by the dates; if they do not
  decide, AIND-D09211 with `low`.

`basis` is `convention` in each of these.

## Names borne by more than one person

| Words | Bearers | How to decide |
|---|---|---|
| «الشرف المناوي» | AIND-D10815 (died 809); AIND-D10313 (born 798) | By the date of the event (WK 2026-09-15) |
| «المحب بن ظهيرة» | AIND-D01373 (born 789); AIND-D09205 (born 836, died 884) | By date (rule 61 b) |
| «العز بن جماعة» | The grandfather ʿAbd al-ʿAzīz (died 767; no entry, in the registry); the grandson AIND-D07198 (born 749, died 819) | By date. If the date does not decide: `undetermined` (WK 2026-11-05) |
| «التقي» with a family name or alone | al-Fāsī AIND-D06757; Ibn Fahd AIND-D09211; al-Shumunnī AIND-D01494; Ibn Qāḍī ʿAjlūn AIND-D10715; and others | By context. A bare «التقي» with nothing to place it is `undetermined` |
| «ابن المنير» / «المنير» | «ابن المنير»: AIND-D12770. A bare «المنير» is a different designation | Bare «المنير»: `undetermined`, candidates AIND-D12770 and AIND-D01354 (WK 2026-11-05) |
| A bare «العبادي» | al-Sirāj ʿUmar al-ʿAbbādī, AIND-D05841 | Only where he gives an ijāza or teaches, and then `medium`. In any other context `undetermined` (WK 2026-11-09) |
| «ابن المرجوشي» | — | `undetermined`: the nisba index and the «ابن X» index disagree on who is meant (WK 2026-11-09) |

The list is not complete. A father and a son, or a grandfather and a grandson, who share laqab
and family name are the commonest trap; check the generation every time (guideline 5.6).
